PrivCloud is a modern, feature-rich personal cloud storage and remote file management platform designed to provide a centralized hub for storing, organizing, previewing, and sharing digital assets. It operates directly on your machine, giving you total privacy and control over your files from any device.

Connecting your devices is as simple as scanning a QR code. PrivCloud lets you share files safely with password-protected and expiring links, set up collaborative dropboxes for external uploads, stream rich media, and inspect documents right inside the browser.
