PrivCloud is a storage hub that works directly on your computer, giving you one place to keep, sort and find your photos, videos and documents from any phone, tablet or computer. It keeps everything under your control. PrivCloud offers a way to manage your digital life. PrivCloud helps you keep track of your storage. PrivCloud does not require any skills to use.

Connecting your devices is as easy, as scanning a QR code. PrivCloud lets you share files safely with friends and family using password‑protected links. PrivCloud also allows you to set up dropboxes where others can send files directly to you. Designed for users PrivCloud gives a secure and easy way to handle your digital life in one place.
