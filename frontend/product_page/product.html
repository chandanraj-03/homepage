Industry Reality

The Cloud Storage Problem Nobody Talks About

Every month, hundreds of millions of people send money to Google, Apple, Microsoft, and Dropbox just to store their own files. Over time, these costs snowball — and the privacy trade-offs get worse.

💸

Recurring Monthly Fees

Google One, iCloud+, and Dropbox charge ₹249 – ₹2,499 every single month. Over 5 years, that's ₹15,000 – ₹1,50,000+ for storage alone.

📦

Arbitrary Storage Caps

Run out of space? Pay more. Hit a file size limit? Pay more. Need more shared users? Pay higher tier subscriptions indefinitely.

🔍

Privacy & AI Scanning

Your personal photos, confidential contracts, and family videos sit on someone else's servers — scanned, indexed, and potentially used for ad targeting or AI model training.

🔒

Vendor Lock-In

Moving away is deliberately made painful. Your files live in proprietary ecosystems designed to keep you paying. Bulk export tools are intentionally restricted.

🌐

Speed Bottlenecks

Uploading a 4K video or RAW album? You're bottlenecked by your ISP upload speed to a remote data center hundreds of miles away instead of full local LAN speed.

⚠️

Account Suspension Risks

Terms of service change arbitrarily. False automated scans can lock your account. Your critical life data is at the mercy of corporate policy.

Complete File Management & Ingestion

Preserved Hierarchy & Resilient Uploads
  • Drag & Drop Folders: Drag files or complex nested directory trees straight into the browser. Exact folder structures are preserved on disk.
  • Chunked Upload Engine: Multi-gigabyte payloads (10GB–50GB+) are automatically split into resilient streaming chunks to prevent timeout errors.
  • Upload Staging Queue: Review total file counts and payload sizes before batch uploads initiate.
  • Pre-Organized Hubs: Built-in shortcuts for Documents, Movies, Photos, Videos, Projects, and Shared.

Secure File Sharing & Management

Passcodes & Expiration Timers
  • Password-Protected Links: Require recipients to enter a custom password before previewing or downloading files.
  • Configurable Expiration: Set links to automatically expire after 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or Never.
  • Centralized Share Dashboard: View all active links with live countdown timers and 1-click Revoke.
  • Dynamic QR Codes: Every link generates a real-time mobile QR code for instant smartphone camera scanning.
  • No Recipient Accounts: Anyone with the link (and passcode) can download without creating an account.

Privacy-First Architecture

Security & Privacy — Built From the Ground Up

PrivCloud is designed with a zero-knowledge, privacy-first philosophy. Your data is yours — full stop.

🔒 100% Local Storage

Files are stored exclusively on your hard drive and never replicated to external servers.

🛡️ Zero Telemetry

No tracking pixels, no analytics SDKs, and zero telemetry. What you store stays on your PC.

🌐 Encrypted HTTPS Tunnel

Remote connections travel through an encrypted SSL channel without router port forwarding.

🔐 Cryptographic Passwords

Server and link passwords are cryptographically hashed and never stored in plain text.

⏱️ Granular Share Controls

Set custom passcodes, timed auto-expiration, and revoke any share link with 1 click.

🔓 Zero Vendor Lock-In

Files live in standard Windows folders. Browse, copy, or back them up using File Explorer anytime.

Important Information Before You Purchase

Here is exactly what to expect so you get the most out of PrivCloud:

🖥️ PC Must Be Turned On: Your computer acts as the server. When your PC is asleep or off, your cloud cannot be reached. Auto-start ensures it launches on boot.
💾 Storage on Your Hardware: PrivCloud is software. Storage space is provided by your hard drives and external USB pools, governed by your edition cap.
🌐 Remote Access Internet: Remote access needs an active internet connection on the host PC. Local Wi-Fi access works even if the internet drops.
🔄 Seamless In-App Upgrades: Upgrade from Trial → Basic or Basic → Pro directly from within the dashboard without reinstalling.
