Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing and running PrivCloud 3.0.

Is this really a one-time purchase, or are there hidden fees? +
There are absolutely zero recurring fees or hidden subscriptions. Once you purchase your PrivCloud product key, it is yours to use for life with unlimited local storage.
Do I need a dedicated server or special NAS hardware? +
No! PrivCloud runs on your regular Windows laptop or desktop PC. You can store files on your internal SSD or plug in any external USB hard drive.
Can I access my files when I'm away from my home Wi-Fi? +
Yes! PrivCloud includes built-in secure remote tunneling. You can access your dashboard securely from any smartphone, tablet, or laptop anywhere in the world over encrypted HTTPS.
What is the difference between the Basic and Pro tier? +
The Basic edition gives you full cloud functionality with dynamic remote access. The Pro edition adds Permanent Custom Subdomain Reservation (e.g. yourname.loca.lt), priority routing, and VIP lifetime customer support.
Can external clients upload files to me without creating an account? +
Yes! Using PrivCloud's Upload Dropbox feature, you can generate a link and send it to anyone. They can open it on their browser and drag-and-drop files directly to your storage without registering or installing anything.
What happens if I format my computer or switch to a new PC? +
Your product key is tied to your license. You can simply reinstall PrivCloud on your new machine and reactivate your license token.
