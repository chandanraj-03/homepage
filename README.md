**A:** No! PrivCloud runs on your regular Windows laptop or desktop PC. You can store files on your internal drive or plug in any external USB hard drive or SSD.

#### Q: Can I access my files when I'm away from my home Wi-Fi?
**A:** Yes! PrivCloud includes built-in secure remote tunneling. You can access your dashboard securely from any smartphone, tablet, or laptop anywhere in the world.

**A:** The Basic edition gives you full cloud functionality with dynamic remote access. The Pro edition adds **Permanent Custom Subdomain Reservation** (e.g. `yourname.loca.lt`), priority routing, and VIP lifetime support.

**A:** Yes! Using PrivCloud's **Upload Dropbox** feature, you can generate a link and send it to anyone. They can open it on their browser and drag-and-drop files directly to your storage without registering or installing anything.
