## What Is PrivCloud?

PrivCloud is a personal cloud storage solution that transforms your existing Windows computer into a fully featured, self-hosted cloud server. Instead of renting storage space from third-party services like Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud, PrivCloud lets you store, manage, share, and stream your files from your own hardware — accessible from any device, anywhere in the world.

No recurring charges. No storage limits imposed by someone else. **Your computer. Your files. Your cloud.**

---

## The Problem PrivCloud Solves

Every day, millions of people pay monthly fees to store their personal files on someone else's servers. These services come with limitations:

- **Monthly subscription costs** that add up year after year.
- **Storage caps** — you run out of space and must pay more.
- **Privacy concerns** — your personal photos, documents, and videos live on corporate servers outside your control.
- **Internet dependency** — you can't access files locally without downloading them first.
- **Data ownership risks** — terms of service can change, accounts can be locked, and services can shut down.

**PrivCloud eliminates all of these problems.** You pay once, install it on your own PC, and you have a personal cloud that runs on your terms, using storage you already own.

### Important Notes

- **Your computer must be turned on** for others (and you, remotely) to access your cloud. When your PC is off or asleep, the cloud is not accessible. This is the nature of self-hosted storage.
- **Storage capacity** is determined by your edition and your available disk space — PrivCloud does not provide its own storage hardware.
- **Remote access** (Basic and Pro) requires an active internet connection on the host PC. Remote URLs are provided through a secure HTTPS tunneling service.
- **Custom subdomain** (Pro only) gives you a permanent, memorable URL. Basic edition users receive a randomly generated URL that changes each time the server restarts.
- **Product key activation** requires a one-time internet connection during installation. After activation, the license works offline.
- **File sharing links** are accessible to anyone with the link (and password, if set). Treat shared links with the same care as any shared access credential.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to pay every month?**
No. PrivCloud Basic and Pro are one-time purchases with lifetime licenses. There are no subscriptions, renewals, or hidden fees.

**Q: Can I access my files from my phone?**
Yes. Open any web browser on your phone, tablet, or any other device and navigate to the address shown in the PrivCloud Control Panel. The interface automatically adapts to your screen size.

**Q: What happens if my computer is turned off?**
Your cloud is not accessible when your computer is off or in sleep mode. You can enable auto-start so PrivCloud launches whenever your PC boots up.

**Q: Can other people upload files to my cloud?**
With the Pro edition, yes. You can create upload dropboxes — shared folders where others can send files directly to your cloud. Basic and Trial editions support download-only sharing.

**Q: How much storage do I get?**
Your storage is limited by your edition (5 GB Trial, 500 GB Basic, Unlimited Pro) and the available disk space on your computer. PrivCloud uses your existing hard drive — it does not provide separate storage hardware.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
Your files stay on your own computer and are never uploaded to external servers. Access is password-protected, remote connections are encrypted, and you have full control over sharing. As with any important data, we recommend maintaining your own backups.

**Q: Can I move PrivCloud to a different computer?**
Your product key is tied to your license. Contact support for assistance with transferring your license to a new machine.

**Q: What if I want to stop using PrivCloud?**
Uninstall PrivCloud using the included uninstaller. Your files remain on your hard drive — PrivCloud never deletes your stored files during uninstallation unless you explicitly choose to remove the storage folder.

## Before You Begin

Make sure you have the following ready:

- ✅ **A Windows 10 or later computer** — this is the computer that will become your personal cloud server.
- ✅ **At least 125 MB of free disk space** for the installation, plus additional space for the files you plan to store.
- ✅ **An internet connection** — needed during installation to activate your product key.
- ✅ **Your product key** — you received this when you purchased PrivCloud. It looks like this: `XXXXX-XXXXX` (groups of letters and numbers separated by a dash). If you don't have one yet, you can start with a free 14-day trial.

## Step 1 — Run the Installer

1. Find the file called **PrivCloud_Setup.exe** — this is the installer you downloaded.
2. **Double-click** on `PrivCloud_Setup.exe` to start the installation.
3. If Windows shows a security prompt asking *"Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?"*, click **Yes**.

The PrivCloud Setup Wizard will open.

---

## Step 2 — Enter Your Product Key

The first screen of the setup wizard asks for your **product key**.

- **If you have a product key:** Type or paste it into the text box and click the **Activate** button. The installer will verify your key online — this takes a few seconds.
- **If you want to try PrivCloud for free:** Click the option to **Start 14-Day Free Trial** to skip this step and use PrivCloud with limited features for 14 days.

Once your key is accepted (or you choose the trial), click **Next** to continue.

---

## Step 3 — Welcome Screen

This screen provides a brief overview of PrivCloud. Simply click **Next** to continue.

---

## Step 4 — License Agreement

Read the license agreement. To proceed:

1. Select **"I accept the agreement"**.
2. Click **Next**.

You cannot continue without accepting the agreement.

---

## Step 5 — Choose Installation Location

This screen asks where PrivCloud should be installed on your computer.

- **The default location is fine for most users** — it installs to a standard programs folder on your computer.

The wizard will show you how much free space is available on the chosen drive. Make sure you have at least 125 MB free.

### Storage Folder
- This is the folder on your computer where all your cloud files will be stored.
- The default location works well for most users, but you can click **Browse** to choose a different folder — for example, a folder on a larger secondary drive.

### Server Password
- Choose a password that you will use to log into your cloud from a web browser.
- **Remember this password!** You will need it every time you open your cloud from a phone, tablet, or other device.
- You can change this password later from within the application.

### Domain / Subdomain *(Pro Edition only)*

## How to Start Your Cloud Server

1. Open the PrivCloud Control Panel (see above).
2. Click the **Start Server** button.
3. Wait a few seconds — the status will change to show that your server is running.
4. The Control Panel will display your cloud's **web address** (URL) and a **QR code**.

Your personal cloud is now live!

## How to Access Your Cloud from a Web Browser

Once the server is running:

1. **On the same computer:** Open any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) and go to the address shown in the Control Panel — typically something like `http://localhost:9527`.
2. **On your phone or tablet (same Wi-Fi):** Open the camera app and scan the **QR code** displayed in the Control Panel, or type the address shown into your phone's browser.
3. **From anywhere on the internet** *(Basic & Pro editions only)*: Use the remote address shown in the Control Panel — it will look something like `https://your-name.loca.lt`.

When you open the address, you will see a **login screen**. Enter the **server password** you chose during installation.

## How to Uninstall PrivCloud

If you decide to remove PrivCloud from your computer:

1. Find the **"Uninstall"** file in the PrivCloud installation folder, or search for **"PrivCloud Uninstall"** in the Start Menu.
2. Double-click it to open the uninstaller.
3. The uninstaller will show you what will be removed.
4. **Important:** You will be asked if you also want to delete your **storage folder** (the folder containing all your uploaded files). 
   - Choose **No** if you want to keep your files.
   - Choose **Yes** only if you are sure you want to permanently delete all stored files. You will need to type a confirmation phrase to proceed.
5. Click **Uninstall** and wait for the process to complete.

The uninstaller will:
- Stop any running PrivCloud processes.
- Remove desktop shortcuts and Start Menu entries.
- Remove auto-start entries (if configured).
- Remove firewall rules (if configured).
- Delete the application files.

**Your stored files are NOT deleted** unless you specifically chose to remove them in step 4.

## Common Issues & Solutions

### "I forgot my password"
If you cannot remember the password you set during installation, you will need to open the PrivCloud Control Panel on the computer where PrivCloud is installed. From the **Storage & Security** tab, you can view or change the password. If you cannot access the Control Panel, reinstalling PrivCloud will let you set a new password.

### "My phone can't find the cloud"
- Make sure your phone and the PrivCloud computer are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
- Make sure the PrivCloud server is **running** (check the Control Panel — it should say "Running" or show a green status).
- Try typing the full address manually into your phone's browser instead of scanning the QR code.
- Make sure you allowed PrivCloud through the **Windows Firewall** during installation.

### "I see a Windows Firewall warning"
If Windows shows a firewall alert when you start the server for the first time, click **Allow access**. This lets other devices on your network connect to your cloud. If you block it, only the computer running PrivCloud can access the cloud.

### "The remote/internet address isn't working"
- Remote access requires a **Basic or Pro license** — the free trial only works over local Wi-Fi.
- Make sure your computer has an **active internet connection**.
- The remote address may take a few seconds to become available after starting the server.
- If you're using the Basic edition, your remote address **changes every time you restart** the server. Copy the new address from the Control Panel after each restart.

### "Upload failed" or "Storage limit reached"
- Check if your storage quota has been reached. You can see your usage in the PrivCloud dashboard or Control Panel.
- The free trial has a 5 GB storage limit and 250 MB per-file upload limit.
- Free up space by deleting files you no longer need, or upgrade your license for more storage.

### "The installer asks for a product key but I want the free trial"
Look for the **"Start Free Trial"** or **"Try for 14 Days"** option on the product key screen. You do not need a product key to try PrivCloud.

### "PrivCloud isn't starting automatically"
- Open the Control Panel and check if the **auto-start option** is enabled in the Additional Options.
- If it was enabled during installation, try disabling it and re-enabling it from the Control Panel.

### "Can I run PrivCloud on Mac or Linux?"
PrivCloud is currently designed for **Windows** only. macOS and Linux are not supported at this time.
