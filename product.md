## What Is PrivCloud?

PrivCloud is **personal cloud storage software** that transforms your existing Windows PC into a fully featured, self-hosted cloud server.

Instead of uploading your files to a remote corporate data center, PrivCloud keeps everything **on your own hard drive** — and makes it accessible from any device, any browser, anywhere in the world.

**You get the convenience of Dropbox, the media power of Google Photos, and the privacy of a personal vault — without paying a single monthly fee.**

### How It Works (Simple Overview)

1. **Install PrivCloud** on your Windows PC (takes under 3 minutes).
2. **Choose a storage folder** — any folder on any drive your PC can access.
3. **Start the server** with one click from the desktop Control Panel.
4. **Access your cloud** from any phone, tablet, laptop, or PC — via any web browser.
5. **Share files, stream media, and manage your storage** from the browser-based dashboard.

Your computer becomes the server. Your hard drive becomes the cloud. **You own everything.**

## Security & Privacy — Built From the Ground Up

PrivCloud is designed with a **privacy-first, zero-knowledge philosophy**. Your data is yours — full stop.

### 🔒 100% Local File Storage
Your files are stored **exclusively on your own computer's hard drive**. They are never uploaded, synced, mirrored, or replicated to any external cloud server, CDN, or data warehouse. Period.

### 🔒 Zero Telemetry & Zero Data Collection
PrivCloud does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage statistics, file metadata, browsing patterns, or device information. There is no analytics SDK, no tracking pixel, and no phone-home mechanism. What you do on your cloud stays on your cloud.

### 🔒 Encrypted Remote Access
All remote connections use **HTTPS encryption** via secure tunneling. Your files travel through an encrypted channel when accessed from outside your local network.

### 🔒 Cryptographic Password Protection
Server passwords and share link passwords are stored as **cryptographically hashed values** — never in plain text. Even if someone accessed the password storage file, they could not recover the original passwords.

### 🔒 Granular Share Controls
- Set individual passwords on each share link.
- Set automatic expiration timers (1 hour to 30 days).
- Revoke any share link instantly with one click.
- Upload dropboxes are password-protected and quota-aware.

### 🔒 No Vendor Lock-In
Your files are **standard files on your hard drive**. You can browse, copy, move, or back them up using normal Windows tools (File Explorer, robocopy, etc.) at any time. If you uninstall PrivCloud, your files remain exactly where they are. You are never locked in.

# SECTION: Important Notes for Buyers

---

## Important Information Before You Purchase

Please read the following so you know exactly what to expect:

- **Your computer must be turned on** for your cloud to be accessible. When your PC is off, in sleep mode, or hibernating, the cloud cannot be reached by any device (including you, remotely). This is inherent to self-hosted storage.

- **Storage is provided by your own hardware.** PrivCloud does not include or provide any physical storage. Your storage capacity is determined by your edition limits (5 GB Trial, 500 GB Basic, Unlimited Pro) AND the available space on your hard drive — whichever is smaller.

- **Remote access requires an internet connection** on the host PC. If your internet goes down, remote access is temporarily unavailable (local Wi-Fi access still works).

- **Basic edition URLs change on each restart.** Every time you restart your server, you receive a new randomly generated HTTPS address. Pro edition includes a permanent custom URL that never changes.

- **Product key activation requires a one-time internet connection** during installation. After activation, your license works fully offline — no periodic check-ins or renewals.

- **Shared links are accessible to anyone** who has the link (and password, if set). Treat share links with the same care as any access credential.

- **PrivCloud currently supports Windows only.** macOS and Linux are not supported at this time.

- **Upgrading is seamless.** You can upgrade from Trial to Basic, or from Basic to Pro, directly from within the application — no reinstallation required.
